# Submit Your Work

When the checker score is as high as you can get it, submit.

```
bash /root/grading/submit_serveros.sh YOUR_GROUP_ID YOUR_GITHUB_REPO_URL
```

This captures your fixed configuration, the checker result, your incident log
and session recording, and pushes them to your group's repository. Make sure
your incident log and AI critique are committed too (see the handout).
